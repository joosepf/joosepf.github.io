USB–CAN FD adapter (DIY_KVASER)USB-to-CAN FD adapter (DIY_KVASER)

STM32STM32CCFreeRTOSFreeRTOSCAN FDCAN FD

EesmärkGoal

Anda meeskonnale odav USB–CAN-adapter, mis töötab ilma eraldi draiverita nii Windowsis kui ka Linuxis.

Give the team a low-cost USB-to-CAN adapter that works without extra drivers on both Windows and Linux.

KirjeldusDescription

Püsivara meeskonna STM32G431-põhisele plaadile. See rakendab gs_usb/candleLighti protokolli, nii et Linuxis töötab adapter tuuma draiveriga (SocketCAN) ja Windowsis paigaldub WinUSB kaudu automaatselt. Toetab CAN 2.0 ja CAN FD-d bitikiiruse vahetusega; kontrollisin pingil Kvaser Memorator Pro vastu kõiki klassikalisi kiirusi 10 kbit/s – 1 Mbit/s ja CAN FD andmefaasi kuni 8 Mbit/s. Porditud projektist budgetcan-fw (FreeRTOS).

Firmware for the team's STM32G431-based board. It implements the gs_usb/candleLight protocol, so the adapter works with the Linux kernel driver (SocketCAN) and installs itself on Windows through WinUSB. It supports CAN 2.0 and CAN FD with bit-rate switching; I verified it on the bench against a Kvaser Memorator Pro at every classic rate from 10 kbit/s to 1 Mbit/s and with CAN FD data phases up to 8 Mbit/s. Ported from budgetcan-fw (FreeRTOS).
