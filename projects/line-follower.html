EesmärkGoal

Luua lihtne joonejälgija roboti katsetamiseks.

Build a simple line follower to experiment with the robot.

KirjeldusDescription

Robotiplatvormi edasiarendus, mille peamine eesmärk on kasutada robotil olevaid andureid ning mootorite vahelist koostööd. Selle jaoks on koostatud lihtne joonejälgimisprogramm.

An extension of the robot platform whose main goal is to use the robot's onboard sensors and the coordination between its motors. A simple line-following program was written for this.
