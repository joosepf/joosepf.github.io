KirjeldusDescription

Programmi sisendiks on erinevate müüjate andmed, mille põhjal arvutatakse igaühe müügitulemused ja statistika. Loodud aine „Programmeerimine I“ raames.

The program takes data from various salespeople as input and computes each one's sales results and statistics. Created for the “Programming I” course.
